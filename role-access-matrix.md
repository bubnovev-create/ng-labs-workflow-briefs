| Role | May view | May act | Must not access | Who grants/removes access | Refusal example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer | Their own request | Submit a draft | Another customer’s records | To agree | Attempt another customer’s record |
| Reviewer | Assigned work | Ask for correction | Unassigned work or final approval without authority | To agree | Attempt an approval |
| Approver | Current version in remit | Record an agreed decision | Unrelated work | To agree | Attempt an old version |
| Operator | Agreed operational context | Investigate unresolved work | Unrestricted access by default | To agree | Act after permission is removed |

For each row, record account invitation, role changes, old links, active sessions and information already downloaded. Decide which changes require a new review. This table is a requirements aid, not proof of implemented authorization.
